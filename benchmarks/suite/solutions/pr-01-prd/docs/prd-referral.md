# PRD: Referral program

## Goals
- Grow sign-ups through invitations at a lower cost per sign-up than ads.

## Non-goals
- Mobile app support in the first release.

## Users
- Existing customers who invite friends; invited friends who sign up.

## Requirements
- Each customer has a personal invite link.
- When an invited friend signs up and completes a first order, both receive a 10% discount on their next order.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a customer, when they open their account page, then they see their personal invite link.
- Given an invited friend completed a first order, then both accounts hold one 10% discount.
- Given a sign-up that uses the inviter's own email or payment card, then no reward is given (self-referral).

## Success metrics
- Cost per sign-up from referrals is at least 30% lower than from ads after one quarter.
